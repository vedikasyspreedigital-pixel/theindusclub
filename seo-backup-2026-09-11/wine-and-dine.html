Wine & Dine

The Taste of Success

FOOD

Our members have a refined idea of gourmet. So, to satisfy a palate that is demanding and varied we have a multi-cuisine spread specially designed by Celebrity Chef Ajay Chopra. Our team of exemplary chefs serves up four specialty cuisines - Modern Indian, Pan-Asian, European, and Middle Eastern. Members have a choice of dining spaces - Caesar’s, the Fine Dine Restaurant, Riviera, the Casual Dining Lounge, the Sinatra Bar, and the Private Dining Rooms: Warren Buffett and Michelin. And the piece de resistance, the magnificent Skye Lounge.

COCKTAILS

For those who enjoy a good cocktail, our mixologists and expert bartenders are highly-skilled in whipping up the best in legendary classics as well as original creations.

WHISKIES & SINGLE MALTS

From the sweetness of the highlands to the floral tones of the lowlands or the smoky notes of the islands, we offer some of the finest and rarest spirits from around the world exclusively to members and their guests.

WINES

With only the best wines handpicked for our members, we ensure the right balance of exceptional taste and score. Wine enthusiasts and sommeliers will appreciate that only wines which score rating of 85 points* and above are included in our list.

*Source: Wine Spectator

CIGARS

For members who are true connoisseurs or for those just beginning a journey of discovery, our cigar collection offers international varieties which are unsurpassed.
