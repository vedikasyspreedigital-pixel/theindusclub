A Private Theatre for Members

For those who appreciate cinema, entertainment and storytelling, The Indus Club offers a private theatre designed for an exclusive viewing experience.

The theatre creates opportunities for members to enjoy films, screenings and other customized experiences within the privacy of the Indus Club. Removed from the scale and anonymity of a conventional cinema, it offers a setting where entertainment becomes a shared occasion among a familiar fraternity.

A private screening can be followed by dinner. Conversation about a film can continue over drinks. A special programme can become the centrepiece of an evening without the usual distractions of a public venue.

For those in the quest for a club with private theatre in Mumbai, The Indus Club brings together cinema, private association and a lifestyle experience at a single destination in Bandra Kurla Complex.
